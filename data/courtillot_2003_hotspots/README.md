      # courtillot_2003_hotspots — Courtillot 2003 primary hotspots (Table 1)

      **Provenance:** Courtillot, Davaille, Besse & Stock (2003)

      Present-day locations of the seven 'primary' deep-mantle hotspots (Hawaii, Iceland, Louisville, Réunion, Tristan, Afar, Easter) identified by Courtillot et al. (2003) using their five criteria (age progression, LIP link, radial anomaly at 1000+ km depth, high 3He/4He, buoyancy flux).
