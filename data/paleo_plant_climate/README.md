# paleo_plant_climate — Changhsingian and Induan plant fossils and surface-air temperature

**Provenance:** Xu et al. (2025), *Nature Communications*; author-contributed by Zhen Xu and Benjamin J.W. Mills

Plant-fossil occurrences of the Changhsingian (latest Permian) and Induan (earliest Triassic), with present-day coordinates and a plant-functional-type code (`Function`), and the matching surface-air-temperature fields of the two stages from the climate modelling of Xu et al. (2025).

## Contents

```
paleo_plant_climate/  (60 KB)
├── README.md
├── Changhsingian_occurrences.csv   138 occurrences (ID, Country, Area, Function, Lng, Lat, Environment)
├── Induan_occurrences.csv          102 occurrences
├── P_Tair_shifted.csv              Changhsingian surface-air temperature (°C), 40 × 48 grid
├── T_Tair_shifted.csv              Induan surface-air temperature (°C), 40 × 48 grid
├── clima_lat.csv                   the 40 grid latitudes
└── clima_lon_shifted.csv           the 48 grid longitudes
```
