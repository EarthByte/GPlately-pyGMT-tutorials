# manganese_deposits — Manganese deposits through 1.8 Ga

**Provenance:** EarthByte/Manganese-paleogeography (https://github.com/EarthByte/Manganese-paleogeography); Müller et al. (in review, *Geology*)

Global compilation of manganese deposits, their reconstructed paleolatitudes on the Cao et al. (2024) model, and 5- and 10-Myr binned deposit counts, taken from the EarthByte/Manganese-paleogeography repository, which holds the full workflow. Used by K07.
