        # paleo_elevation_zhou — Zhou geochemistry-corrected paleo-elevation maps

        **Provenance:** Zhou et al. — assimilation into Scotese-Wright paleo-DEMs

        Paleo-elevation grids corrected by geochemistry-derived crustal-thickness/elevation samples (Brown 2022, Condie 2016, Davis 2009 age-temperature compilations), plus the raw sample CSVs used in the assimilation. Corrects the top-1% land amplitude bias in the uncorrected Scotese-Wright 2018 paleo-DEMs (2 km → 4 km modern reference).
