# LIPS_volcanic_chains — Johansson et al. (2018) volcanic provinces

**Provenance:** Johansson, Zahirovic & Müller (2018), EarthByte

Global compilation of large igneous provinces and other volcanic provinces (age-progressive and non-age-progressive) in GPlates format, version 2 of the Johansson et al. (2018) dataset. `README.txt` is the dataset's original EarthByte readme, with its version notes and the citations its authors request. E02 reconstructs the provinces younger than 130 Ma.
